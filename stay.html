Where to stay: pick an area, not just a hotel

The area you choose changes your daily transport cost, your meal budget and how much you will actually see. Decide the area first, then compare properties inside it.

Six questions to ask about any area

  1. What is the door-to-door trip time from the airport or main station, at the hour you will actually arrive?
  2. What runs after midnight? If nothing does, what is your fallback ride home and what does it cost?
  3. What is the noise profile? Read reviews specifically for night noise, thin walls, and bars or clubs nearby.
  4. How steep is it? Cobbles, stairs and long hills change your plans on a hot day.
  5. How do you eat? Is there food nearby after 21:00, or will you be relying on one expensive restaurant?
  6. Is it flexible? Free cancellation is worth more than a small discount when plans change.

Quick checklist

  • Check the late check-in policy and how to reach the door after midnight
  • Request a quiet room away from lifts, stairs and bars
  • Confirm luggage storage before and after your stay
  • Confirm whether breakfast, taxes and resort fees are included in the total
  • Save the address in the local language and take a screenshot

How to book this

  1. Shortlist 2–3 options with free cancellation.
  2. Check area noise, late check-in and luggage rules.
  3. Compare refundable vs. non-refundable totals, including fees.
  4. Save the confirmation offline and set a cancellation reminder.
