Start here: how to plan any trip

Entry rules, health requirements and prices change constantly — always confirm with official government and provider sources. This page gives you an order of operations so you do not miss the things that are hard to fix later.

The order that actually matters

  1. Passport and visas. Check validity, blank pages and visa rules for every country on your route, including transit stops.
  2. Flights first. Flights fix your dates and your arrival time; everything else should follow from them.
  3. First night of accommodation. Book it with free cancellation. The rest can wait.
  4. Daily budget and area. Set a tier and choose the area before booking anything else.
  5. Only then: paid activities, and only the ones that sell out or need a date.

Money basics

  • Notify your bank before you travel so a foreign payment does not get frozen.
  • Carry one card with no foreign transaction fee as your backup, and keep it separate from your everyday card.
  • Cash acceptance varies enormously. Keep small local notes for markets, tips and rural transport.
  • Use bank ATMs inside branches, and always decline the machine’s own currency conversion offer.
  • Learn to say “the local currency, please” before you travel — it saves money at every exchange counter.

Connectivity

Pick an eSIM for setup before you fly, or a local SIM at the airport for cheaper data. Registration usually needs a passport, so keep it with you. Typical starter packs cover 5–10 GB — check whether your plan includes roaming before you buy anything.

Health, insurance and entry

  • Check required and recommended vaccinations 4–8 weeks before departure.
  • Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment, cancellation and lost luggage.
  • Save the local emergency number for each country on your route.
  • Check whether your medicines are legal at your destination and carry them in original packaging.

Quick checklist

  • Photocopies or offline scans of passports and visas
  • Travel insurance policy number saved offline
  • Offline copies of every booking confirmation
  • Emergency contacts and the local emergency number
  • Two forms of the plan: one on paper, one in the cloud

How to book this

  1. Shortlist 2–3 options with free cancellation.
  2. Check area noise, late check-in and luggage rules.
  3. Compare refundable vs. non-refundable totals, including fees.
  4. Save the confirmation offline and set a cancellation reminder.
