How this guide works

This site is deliberately destination-agnostic. Nothing here assumes a specific country, currency or language — instead it gives you the questions to ask and the method to apply them in the place you picked.

  • Set a daily tier (shoestring, budget, mid, comfort) and treat it as your baseline, not a target.
  • Pick your area before you pick your accommodation; it changes every other cost.
  • Check the local fare rules, emergency number and entry rules for your destination before you fly.
  • Compare like with like: total door-to-door time, refundable totals, and fees.

Coverage: Any destination. Prices are indicative and refreshed periodically — confirm anything you rely on with the provider.

Quick checklist

  • Check passport validity — most countries require 6+ months
  • Confirm visa or entry rules for every country on your route
  • Carry a card with no foreign transaction fee
  • Download offline maps and save booking confirmations
  • Store your accommodation address in the local language

How to book this

  1. Shortlist 2–3 options with free cancellation.
  2. Check area noise, late check-in and luggage rules.
  3. Compare refundable vs. non-refundable totals, including fees.
  4. Save the confirmation offline and set a cancellation reminder.
