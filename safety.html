Safety and etiquette

Almost every travel problem comes from a handful of predictable situations. Learn the local rules before you arrive and most of them stop being problems.

Before you travel

Save the local emergency number for every country on your route before you fly. Many places also list a tourist police line.

  • Store your embassy or consulate contact details offline.
  • Share your itinerary with someone who is not travelling with you.
  • Keep passport copies, insurance details and booking references in two places.
  • Know where the nearest hospital or clinic to your accommodation is.

Common scams

    A general list rather than a destination-specific one. Search your destination plus “tourist scam” before you travel for local variations.

    Habits that remove most risk

    • Use licensed transport or a well-established ride-hailing app; agree the price first.
    • Buy tickets from official counters, official apps or authorised resellers only.
    • Carry a card and a separate small amount of cash, in different places.
    • Keep your phone charged and install offline maps before you go out at night.
    • Watch local flag and beach warning systems rather than assuming they mean what you expect.
    • Walk in well-lit, populated streets at night, especially for the first few nights in a new city.

    Etiquette that gets you a better reception

      Etiquette varies widely. When in doubt, watch what local people do first, and expect “no problem” or a smile to mean yes rather than no.

      Quick checklist

      • Share your itinerary and accommodation details with a friend at home
      • Keep copies of IDs and the insurance policy number offline
      • Save the local emergency number in your phone, not just in your notes app
      • Check weather warnings and local alerts each morning

      How to book this

      1. Shortlist 2–3 options with free cancellation.
      2. Check area noise, late check-in and luggage rules.
      3. Compare refundable vs. non-refundable totals, including fees.
      4. Save the confirmation offline and set a cancellation reminder.
