How to book

Booking is where most people overspend, because they compare the headline price instead of the total cost. A few habits fix almost all of it.

Compare the total, not the fare

  • Include baggage, seat selection, taxes, resort or city fees and card surcharges in every quote
  • Compare refundable against non-refundable totals, not the price difference alone
  • Check whether the price is per person and whether single supplements apply
  • Compare door-to-door time, including transfers, waiting and walking
  • Check change and cancellation deadlines, which are often earlier than departure

Flights

  1. Search 4–8 weeks ahead as a baseline; earlier for peak season or long-haul.
  2. Check the local school holiday calendar and public holidays before you fix dates.
  3. Verify that names match passports exactly, including middle names.
  4. Decide your baggage allowance before you book, not at check-in.
  5. Avoid tight separate bookings on the same day unless you have a long buffer and a backup.

Trains and buses

  • Book advance for high-speed or sleeper services; walk-up pricing is often higher.
  • Check whether a seat reservation is mandatory or optional on your route.
  • Look for off-peak or advance-purchase fares — they are usually the single biggest saving available.
  • Confirm the platform or departure time close to travel, since it can change.

Stay

  1. Choose the area first — central, old-town, coastal, quiet, nightlife or airport-side.
  2. Check late arrival instructions and ask for a quiet room.
  3. Use maps to confirm walk times to what you actually plan to do.
  4. Check check-in and check-out times; early arrival often means paying for a night you do not use.
  5. Free cancellation is worth more than a small discount when plans may change.

Connectivity

Choose between an eSIM and a local SIM. Keep your passport for registration, and compare the per-gigabyte cost over your whole stay, not just the headline data amount. Check whether your existing plan already includes roaming before you buy anything.

Quick checklist

  • Save every confirmation offline before you travel
  • Set price alerts on the one or two bookings that matter most
  • Set calendar reminders for cancellation deadlines
  • Check the final total one more time before you pay
