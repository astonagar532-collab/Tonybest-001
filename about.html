About Travel & Destinations

Travel & Destinations is a small, fast planning guide for people who want to travel more and spend less. It is deliberately destination-agnostic: instead of one city’s information that dates quickly, it gives you the method, the questions and the budget baselines that transfer to any trip.

What makes it different

  • No invented precision. We publish ranges, typical figures and the date they were checked — not fake “exact” prices.
  • One base currency. Everything is anchored to USD and converted, so nothing breaks when you travel outside the country the guide was written in.
  • Total cost, not headline price. Fees, transfers and time are part of the comparison.
  • Accessible by default. The site is keyboard-navigable, respects reduced-motion settings and prints cleanly.
  • Works offline. No build step, no tracking scripts, no paywall.

Editorial standards

  • Original writing, reviewed regularly
  • Indicative prices with the date they were last updated
  • No “best” or “cheapest” claims — we say “typical” and show the trade-off
  • No scraped or rewritten third-party content
  • Examples are clearly labelled as illustrative patterns rather than recommendations
