Privacy

This is a plain-language template. It is not legal advice — have it reviewed for your jurisdiction before publishing.

What this site stores

Travel & Destinations is a static site with no account system. It does not ask for your name, email address or any personal detail.

  • localStorage: remembers which budget tier tab you last opened, on your device only.
  • sessionStorage: remembers whether you have already dismissed the notice dialog, for the current session only.
  • Budget calculator export: anything you download is generated in your browser and saved wherever you choose.

None of this is sent to a server, and clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Third parties

Some outbound links go to booking, mapping or operator sites. Once you follow one of those links, that provider’s own privacy policy applies to you, and this site has no control over what they collect.

Analytics and advertising

This template ships without analytics or ad scripts. If you add any, update this page and the affiliate disclosure before you publish.

Your choices

Because nothing is linked to an account, there is nothing to export or delete on our side. To remove the stored tab preference, clear site data for this domain in your browser settings.
